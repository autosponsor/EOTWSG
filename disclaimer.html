General education

Use current, local, and professional guidance.

EOTWSG provides general educational information. It does not provide emergency, medical, legal, mental-health, engineering, or professional safety advice, and it cannot account for local conditions or individual needs.

During an emergency, follow local authorities and emergency services. For medical questions, medications, accessibility needs, or health emergencies, seek guidance from qualified professionals and current public-health resources.

Preparedness plans should be reviewed and adapted for household members, local hazards, disabilities, children, older adults, pets, and changing conditions.
